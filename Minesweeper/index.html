Click to clear, right-click to flag, and click a number once its flags match. Arrows move, Enter clears, F flags, U undoes, H hints.

How to play

Clear every cell that is not a mine. The first cell you open is always safe, and if nothing touches it, the empty area around it opens too.

  • Numbers count the mines in the eight cells around them. Use that count to decide which neighbors are safe and which are mines.
  • Flags mark a cell you think is a mine. Right-click cycles through a flag, a question mark, and blank. On a phone, long-press, or turn on Flag and tap. The mine counter drops once for each flag.
  • Opening around a number works once the flags next to it match the number. Click the number and the other neighbors open. A flag on the wrong cell sets off that mine.
  • You win when every safe cell is open. Opening a mine ends the game. The clock starts on your first clear, and your best time is saved for each difficulty. A game you used a hint on does not count.
